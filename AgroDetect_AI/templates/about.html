About AgroDetect AI

Learn about the technology powering our plant disease detection system

What is Transfer Learning?

Transfer learning is a machine learning technique where a model trained on one task is reused as the starting point for a different but related task. Instead of training a neural network from scratch, we leverage knowledge learned from millions of images to quickly adapt to our specific plant disease detection task.

This approach allows us to achieve high accuracy with less training data and computational resources, making advanced AI accessible for agricultural applications.

MobileNetV2 Architecture

AgroDetect AI uses MobileNetV2, a state-of-the-art convolutional neural network designed for mobile and embedded vision applications. Key features include:

  • Efficient Design: Optimized for speed and accuracy with minimal computational requirements
  • Pre-trained Weights: Trained on ImageNet dataset with 1.4 million images across 1,000 categories
  • Custom Layers: We added specialized layers on top to classify 38 plant disease categories
  • High Accuracy: Achieves 92-96% validation accuracy on plant disease classification

Dataset Information

Our model is trained on the New Plant Diseases Dataset from Kaggle, which contains:

  • Over 87,000 high-quality images of healthy and diseased plant leaves
  • 38 different classes covering multiple crops including tomato, potato, corn, grape, apple, and more
  • Images captured under various lighting conditions and angles for robust learning
  • Carefully curated and labeled by agricultural experts

How It Works

When you upload an image, here's what happens behind the scenes:

  1. Image Preprocessing: Your image is resized to 224x224 pixels and normalized
  2. Feature Extraction: MobileNetV2 extracts visual features from the image
  3. Classification: Custom layers analyze features to identify the disease
  4. Confidence Score: The model provides a probability score for its prediction
  5. Treatment Lookup: We match the disease to expert-curated treatment recommendations
